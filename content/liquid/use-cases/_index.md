Liquid is a protocol, not an application.

That means its value shows up less as a single "killer app" and more as a collection of simple mechanisms that can be composed into very different social and economic behaviors.

This section collects concrete, real-world use cases that fall naturally out of Liquid's design.

## Personal gift certificates

One of the simplest and most intuitive uses of Liquid is as a **personal gift certificate**—issued by an individual, backed with real tokens, and redeemable at any time.

Unlike traditional gift cards, this mechanism:

- Requires the creator to *heat* the tokens they give away
- Guarantees continuous liquidity via on-chain math
- Encourages spending gently rather than enforcing expiration
- Carries the identity of the giver, not a corporation

The result is a gift that feels personal, honest, and surprisingly effective at being used.

> [A personal gift certificate that encourages spending](/liquid/use-cases/gift-certificates)

## Game currencies

Games already run on currencies—not just gold or gems, but *life*, *energy*, *time*, *risk*, *reputation*, and *control*. These resources are scarce, spent, replenished, and priced against one another.

Liquids provide a way to model these mechanics as explicit, composable currencies rather than hard-coded rules:

- Energy limits throughput and naturally throttles grinding
- Life becomes collateral for high-risk strategies
- Time can be accelerated at rising cost
- Reputation resists inflation through economic enforcement

Instead of asking "Is this balanced?", designers ask "Is this priced correctly?"

> [Liquids as game-native currencies](/liquid/use-cases/game-currencies)

## Liquid-first tokens

For token developers, the most natural use of Liquid may be the most overlooked: make the liquid version the primary token.

The backing token handles everything that isn't liquidity — minting, governance, vesting, access controls. The liquid version handles everything that is — trading, price discovery, cross-token swaps. Users hold one token. Developers get both programmable logic and instant liquidity, composed through a clean interface.

> [Making the liquid version the canonical token](/liquid/use-cases/liquid-first-tokens)
